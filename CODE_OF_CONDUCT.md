# Code of Conduct

We aim to keep this project respectful, practical, and welcoming to engineers and developers of different experience levels.

Participants are expected to:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on technical ideas and implementations.
- Welcome reproducible reports and evidence-based discussion.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks.
- Share only information they are authorized to disclose.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may close participation for repeated or serious violations.
